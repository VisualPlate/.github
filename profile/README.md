# Visual Plate

Explore menus. Discover dishes. Keep your food memories.

Visual Plate is an iOS app that helps you understand dishes on a menu and keep a personal record of the food you discover.

- **Scan menus** with your camera or import a photo.
- **Explore dishes** with photos, descriptions, typical ingredients, and available nutrition notes.
- **Save discoveries** in your personal food library.
- **Share food moments** using your own photos and story templates.
